Yes. Your current backend is already a strong foundation, especially for an exam-focused LMS. You have most of the core bounded contexts: Auth, Course, Enrollment, Exams, Attempts, Questions, Results, Video, Proctoring, Notifications, Storage, Wallet, and Analytics.

For an enterprise-grade LMS, though, I would add a few modules and make some architectural changes before the system grows further.

One important architectural recommendation

Your biggest concern isn't actually "do I need more modules?"

It's boundary ownership.

For example, right now:

VideoClassesService
 ├── CourseManagementService
 ├── CommentService
 └── ProgressTrackingService


I'd be careful here.

A video service shouldn't become a place where everything related to learning gets added.

Prefer:

CourseService
      │
      ├── Course
      ├── Section
      └── Curriculum

ContentService
      │
      ├── Video
      ├── PDF
      └── Lesson

LearningProgressService
      │
      ├── VideoProgress
      ├── PDFProgress
      ├── LessonProgress
      └── CourseProgress

CommunityService
      │
      ├── Comments
      ├── Discussions
      └── Q&A


That's going to keep your architecture much healthier as the product grows.
